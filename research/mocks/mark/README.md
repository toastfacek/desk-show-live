## Construction

72×72 grid, three rectangles, no curves beyond the corner radii on the two masses.

- Desk rule `6,53,60×6`
- BOT1 mass `9,29,32×24` r10 — **1.33:1**, matching `studio.yaml`'s *"width about 1.35 times height"*
- BOT2 mass `46,15,17×38` r8.5 — height clearly greater than width

Both masses sit on the rule. The proportions are what let the two silhouettes separate under the squint test the Character & Set Bible already demands of the hosts themselves.

## Rules

- Clear space: the height of the tall mass, 38 units, on every side.
- Minimum 16 px on screen, 24 px in the overlay. Below 16 the 5-unit gap between the masses closes.
- The masses are **never** coloured orange and cobalt. They are silhouettes, not portraits — painted, they become mascots and the hosts stop being the only place the audience meets them.
- The corner radii stay. The furniture is machined and square; the hosts are soft. That distinction is deliberate.
- No outline version, no gradient, no rotation.

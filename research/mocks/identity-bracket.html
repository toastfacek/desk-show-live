Runtime · identity prototype · revision E

RUNTIME

Two masses at a desk, and the show’s name set once, properly. The previous mark was withdrawn — it had arrived at a construction that already belongs to somebody else.

Why revision C was withdrawn

The bracket had already been taken

Setting a lone lowercase word inside square brackets, in bone on near-black, for a television property, is not a neutral construction. It is the wordmark of an existing cartoon network — one of the most recognised bracket logos there is, and it lives in the same medium this show is going into. That is the worst kind of resemblance: not an abstract echo, but the same shape doing the same job in the same category.

Worth being exact about what collided, because it is narrower than it looks. It is not the bracket. It is the bracket used as an enclosure around a single lowercase word, standing alone as a mark. Every part of that sentence is load-bearing.

Which means the canon device is fine. PHASEONE[lol] is a bracket used as a suffix on a name — an ALLCAPS stem with a small tag hanging off it. Structurally and optically that is a different object, and it reads as an identifier rather than a logo. The bible’s construction was never the problem.

The problem was [runtime], which was not inherited from the bible at all. Nothing in canon puts a bracket around a programme. That was an invention, and it landed on an occupied shape.

The callThe show is called Runtime — that is already canon, and it needs no bracket. The bracket stays exactly where the bible put it: on the hosts’ name bars, self-applied, dating them to their era. The show gets its own mark, drawn from the thing no other network has — this set, with these two on it.

The mark

Two masses on a desk

A broad low form and a narrow tall one, sharing a rule. It is the show’s actual composition reduced until nothing is left but the part that is unmistakably ours: two silhouettes that were designed to be told apart by shape alone, sitting at the one piece of furniture the programme is named for.

Why this one holds

It is not a genre shape. Brackets, terminals, play triangles and signal bars are all occupied several times over; two specific masses at a desk belong to this programme and nothing else. It also survives the squint test the bible already demands of the hosts themselves.

Clear space and minimum

Clear space is the height of the tall mass — 38 units — on every side. Minimum 16 px on screen, 24 px in the overlay. Below 16 the 5-unit gap between the masses closes and they read as one block.

Never

No colouring the masses orange and cobalt. They are silhouettes, not portraits — the moment they are painted they become mascots, and the hosts stop being the only place the audience meets them.

The one colour

The desk is the colour

A package this quiet only gets one saturated element, so it had better mean something. This one does: the rule under the two masses in the mark, and the kicker chip in the lower third, are the same green doing the same job — naming the desk. The show is a desk show; the desk is the brand; the desk is where the colour lives.

Why green

It is the one saturated colour already in the room — the forest pillars either side of the centre wall — taken up to broadcast strength. And crucially neither host owns it. Amber would read as PHASEONE[lol]; blue would read as deb. Green reads as the programme.

Why it survives

7.7:1 on the ground and 7.3:1 on panel, with ink type on the chip rather than bone. Green also sits where chroma subsampling is kindest — unlike saturated blue, which is the first thing a Twitch encode takes apart.

What stays uncoloured

Speaker state, which is value not hue. The ticker’s gain and loss, which stay low-saturation tints so the fully-saturated jade is never in competition. And LIVE, which keeps its own red dot — a state, not the brand.

Sharp nowEvery corner on furniture is square. The radius is gone, not reduced — which also settles the last open token: the spec locks radius-max at 8 px as a maximum, and 0 is inside it. Revision E reopens nothing at all. The only rounded forms left in the package are the two masses in the mark, and they stay rounded because they are portraits of rounded characters — the furniture is machined, the hosts are soft.

The wordmark

Runtime, set once

Archivo 800 at width 84, tracked to −0.03 em. There is no device in it and there does not need to be — the mark carries the identity, so the name can be the quiet half. This is the part where restraint is the whole job.

Where the bracket still lives

On the hosts, and nowhere else

The convention is too good to lose and it is already canon — it just belongs on people, not on the programme. Confined to name bars it does the job the bible describes: a self-applied tag that dates whoever is wearing it, with a guest’s bare serial reading as older and no bracket at all reading as newer.

The code layer

ASCII, and dither with a floor under it

Worth separating these two, because they look related and behave nothing alike. ASCII is type — glyphs at a legible size, which the encoder already handles well and the 480p ladder has already been validated against. Dither is high-frequency noise, which is the single most expensive thing you can hand a video encoder: it steals bitrate from the hosts’ faces and crawls between frames. The spec bans dither by name, and for fine dither it is right to. Coarse dither is a different object, and it is allowed here under two stated numbers.

Cell floor

Nothing textural below 20 px at 1080 — that is the mono cell, and it is the same 26 px-equivalent logic the essential-type floor already uses. The mosaic floor is 8 px cells. Below either number it stops being a pattern and starts being noise.

Where texture lives

Two places, and they follow different rules. The ground wash sits at z=0 under everything and runs permanently, including on air. A field is texture as the subject of the frame, and stays in host-free states: hold, the error card, bumpers, the cold open.

Motion is the gate, not detail

A static pattern encodes once into a reference frame and then costs almost nothing. A moving one cannot be held in reference, so every frame pays again. That is the real line: the wash is static whenever a host is on screen, and drift is reserved for the states where nothing else needs the bits.

Motion is the second gate

A moving dither is far more expensive than a still one — every frame changes at high frequency, so the encoder can hold nothing in reference. That is affordable only where nothing else needs the bits: hold, bumpers, the cold open. It is never affordable behind a host, at any cell size. Drift stays slow — a full cycle measured in tens of seconds, not seconds.

Which texture, when

Two devices, two readings, same rules. ASCII is the machine reporting — use it for hold, errors, anything with a status to state. Dither is the signal settling — use it for bumpers, the cold open, and the graceful version of hold. Never both in one frame.

The overlay

The overlay stops performing

The chamfer was a loud gesture standing in for an idea. With a real idea in the identity, the furniture can go quiet — which is what understated actually costs. It is almost entirely subtraction — with exactly one thing added back.

The inversion is the piece worth arguing for. It replaces hue with value, which means speaker state survives grayscale, every form of colour-vision deficiency and any amount of chroma softening — for free, with no redundant tab propping it up. It is also the highest-contrast element on the canvas at 17.5:1, so the thing the eye should find first is the thing it does find first. The live plate also carries the cursor — the same mark that appears after the wordmark on air — so “live” is one shape wherever it turns up.

Where this leaves it

Nothing blocking

The name is settled

The show is Runtime, which the bible already decided. PHASEONE[show] is out because it puts a character device on a programme and names the show after one of two hosts; [runtime] is out because it lands on a construction an existing network owns. Neither was needed — the bracket does better work on the name bars than it ever did as a logo.

Still open · radius-max

Revision C reopens one token, 14 px against the locked 8, and closes the chamfer, gradient, sheen and edge-light questions revision B opened. glow stays none and now means it. Nothing else in the spec moves.

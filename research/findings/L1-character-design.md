# L1 — Character design: expressive robots with almost no face

## Top picks

### Robot Dreams — simple acting and two-character contrast
- **Source:** https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/robot-dreams-director-interview-simplicity-characters-friendship-1235828307/
- **Steal:** Build the pair from opposite large masses—one low, rounded and bottom-heavy, the other tall, rectangular and sharp-shouldered—then keep their eyes and mouths to circles, dots and one broad stroke.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** The filmmakers explicitly reduced acting to two circles, two dots and one line, sharpened Robot's formerly round design so movement would read, and contrasted Dog's pear body and short legs with Robot's edged torso and long legs. **My visual inference:** for Runtime, the contrast must move upward into head and shoulder shape because the desk and 620×700 crops will hide much of the legs; opposite dominant hues should reinforce, not carry, identity. This directly serves drift tolerance, two-character recognition, flat 2D legibility and 480p survival.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** Two original flat 2D robot hosts with thick, even dark outlines and no fine surface detail. One has a low rounded head, broad sloping shoulders and a warm orange dominant shell; the other has a tall clipped-rectangle head, narrow square shoulders and a cool cyan dominant shell, so either remains identifiable as a solid silhouette in a waist-up crop.

### WALL-E — binocular eyes as the whole performance
- **Source:** https://collider.com/walle-q-a-with-writerdirector-andrew-stanton/
- **Steal:** Treat the eye pair as one hinged graphic unit whose spacing, inward cant and whole-head tilt create the expression, without adding a nose or mouth.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** Andrew Stanton describes making binoculars read as happy, sad and mad by tipping their central hinge, explicitly noting that the object has no nose or mouth. **My visual inference:** large paired lenses offer a few coarse, repeatable emotional states that remain readable after compression, while small redraw errors look like intentional lens articulation. This serves drift tolerance, eye-led acting, 620×700 crops and 480p.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** A mouthless robot face made from two large solid lens shapes joined as one movable eye assembly. Show emotion only through three bold variables—eyes level or canted, pupils centered or strongly offset, and the whole head tilted—using changes large enough to read at thumbnail size.

### Big Hero 6's Baymax — radical facial reduction backed by body shape
- **Source:** https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/oscars-big-hero-6-filmmaker-773850/
- **Steal:** Pair an extremely reduced two-dot face with one unmistakable soft body mass and let the physical silhouette establish temperament before the face moves.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** Director Don Hall identifies the design as two eyes, a pear-shaped body and stumpy legs, and connects the calm face motif to the character's nature. **My visual inference:** a thick bridge rather than a hairline connector is the compression-safe adaptation, and Runtime should borrow the economy rather than the specific pale inflatable body. This serves no-mouth expression, strong silhouette, drift tolerance and small-screen legibility.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** An original friendly robot with a broad dark face panel containing only two bold circular eye lights joined by a short thick bridge, with no lips, teeth or nose. Give the shell one large, simple, nonhuman mass and let a clear head dip or lean carry the emotional beat.

### Robot Dreams' ligne-claire production approach — flat fields and limited shadows
- **Source:** https://www.screendaily.com/features/how-robot-dreams-director-pablo-berger-made-a-dialogue-free-love-letter-to-new-york-/5189245.article
- **Steal:** Use continuous clean contour lines, flat colors and almost no shadow, then seek restrained, truthful poses rather than constant facial motion.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** Pablo Berger states that the hand-drawn production retained continuous clean lines, flat colors and limited shadows, and deliberately toned down exaggerated acting. **My visual inference:** broad held poses give a chained five-second clip fewer details to mutate, while uniform contours and unmodulated fills survive Twitch encoding better than painterly shading. This serves heavy linework, flat color, low frame counts, drift tolerance and 480p.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** Flat hand-drawn 2D animation with heavy uniform contour lines, three broad color fields per character, hard-edged fills and at most one large shadow shape. Hold the torso in a clear conversational pose while only the eyes, brow plate and head angle make slow, deliberate changes; no texture, hatching, thin highlights or gradients.

### UPA cartoon modernism — design for selective redrawing
- **Source:** https://collections.libraries.indiana.edu/IULMIA/exhibits/show/nick-and-stephen-bosustow-film/united-productions-of-america-
- **Steal:** Reduce characters to hard-edged geometry and bold unmodulated color, redrawing only the part that communicates the beat while the rest holds.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** Indiana University's archive describes simplified shapes, bold colors without rounding or shadow, and limited animation at twelve, eight or six drawings per second with only moving portions redrawn. **My visual inference:** a stable three-mass construction—head, torso, forearms—makes model drift less conspicuous because local motion does not demand global reshaping. This directly serves heavy linework, flat fields, low frame counts, drift tolerance and compression.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** Geometric limited-animation characters built from three large interlocking masses, outlined with one consistent heavy stroke and filled with bold unmodulated colors. Keep most of each pose still and isolate expression to one coarse change at a time: a brow plate rotates, eye blocks squash, or the head shifts by a few degrees.

### Autumn Robot Animation System — simplify first, then make modular states
- **Source:** https://martinaltanie.com/autumn
- **Steal:** Remove optional anatomy and define a small reusable library of face-and-pose states instead of adding detail to create emotional range.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** The case study reports that stakeholder tests favored the simpler robot, that hands were removed, and that expression was expanded through the screen face and reusable animated states. **My visual inference:** Runtime should similarly constrain each host to a neutral state plus a few gross variants—listen, agree, doubt, laugh—whose differences are in eye geometry and head angle, not fragile ornament. This serves drift tolerance, screen-face acting, consistent chained clips and 480p.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** A simple mouthless screen-faced robot with no fingers, cables or small accessories, using a dark visor and two oversized solid eye glyphs. Expressions come from a tiny repeatable state library: neutral level eyes, cheerful upward eye arcs, skeptical unequal eye heights, and laughter as compressed eyes plus a backward head tilt.

### MiRAE minimalist robot-face study — sparse cues plus neck posture
- **Source:** https://www.caseybennett.com/uploads/MiRAE_Paper_Final.pdf
- **Steal:** Allocate expressive degrees of freedom to a few large eye/brow marks and head tilt, because posture can amplify a face without adding facial detail.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** In a study of 75 participants, the minimalist face achieved expression-identification rates comparable to more complex robots; adding neck motion raised identification to 100% for all tested expressions except fear, although that neck-motion condition had only 15 participants. **My inference:** the result supports using conspicuous head posture as redundant signaling, not promising exact emotion-recognition rates for a cartoon stream. This serves eye-led expression, no-mouth acting, low detail and drift tolerance.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** Use only two large eyes, two thick mechanical brow plates and clear head posture, with no realistic mouth. Make each emotion redundant across coarse cues: curiosity is pupils to one side plus a head lean, doubt is one raised brow plate plus unequal eye heights, and amusement is narrowed eyes plus a small backward tilt.

### Production model sheets — invariants that survive many hands
- **Source:** https://animationresources.org/design-reluctant-dragon-and-pinocchio-model-sheets/
- **Steal:** Distill each approved character into a compact sheet of construction rules, key angles, expressions and poses that identifies what may vary and what must not.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** Animation Resources describes approved designs being distilled into sheets covering a full range of angles and expressions, with construction and posing instructions distributed to every artist. **My inference:** a video model cannot obey a sheet as reliably as trained artists, but a short invariant list is the closest practical analogue: dominant head shape, shoulder width, face-panel shape, eye count and dominant color should never change, while small seams and proportions may drift. This directly addresses the five-second redraw problem and two-character continuity.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** Preserve five identity anchors in every frame: the exact dominant head silhouette, shoulder-width category, face-panel shape, number and placement of eyes, and one dominant shell color. Allow only minor seam placement and secondary proportions to vary; keep all identity anchors bold, simple and visible above the desk in a waist-up crop.

## Avoid

- **Two near-identical capsule mascots separated only by hue.** Twitch compression, color-vision differences and changing generated light can collapse color contrast; each host needs a different head-and-shoulder silhouette in grayscale.
- **Thin LED equalizers, tiny dot-matrix mouths or subtle pupil changes.** They are tempting mouth substitutes, but one-pixel bars, fine dither and low-amplitude waveforms will shimmer or disappear at a 480p half-frame upscale.
- **Hinged jaws, teeth and phoneme-shaped lip sync.** A jaw creates another high-salience contour for the model to deform every five seconds, making drift look like broken anatomy; speech should read through head rhythm, eyes and optional single-block status shapes.
- **Exposed mechanics, cable hair, finger-heavy gesturing and dense panel seams.** These generate many unstable correspondences across chained clips, clutter the silhouette and turn compression noise into apparent design changes.
- **Photoreal metal, transparent glass visors and gradient-dependent facial displays.** Specular highlights and reflections move independently of the intended acting and expose temporal inconsistency; flat opaque shells and solid face marks hide it.

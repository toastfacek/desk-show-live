# L2 — The set: a TV studio built inside a startup office

## Top picks

### SLV Console (CGI)
- **Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/190984217/SLV-Console-(CGI)
- **Steal:** Use two or three mismatched, deep-bodied CRTs as chunky objects, with worn painted metal, dusty recesses, thick knobs, and loose cable loops supplying the texture; keep every screen to dim raster glow, broad waveform-like bands, or soft static rather than interface detail. The source documents its CRT shadow mask, distortion, flicker, chipped paint, dust, and modular construction; the recommendation to simplify those effects is my inference for Runtime.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** Large housings and broad luminance patches satisfy constraint 3 (legibility at 620×700 and 480p) and constraint 4 (survival through Twitch encoding), while abstract screen states satisfy constraint 5 (no readable text). **Visual inference:** a little screen bloom and one broad scan band will communicate “CRT” after downsampling more reliably than the source's fine RGB mask.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** Two or three mismatched deep-backed tube monitors sit off-centre in worn cream and charcoal housings, surrounded by thick dials, blunt switches, dusty seams, and a few heavy cable loops. Their glass shows only low-contrast static, broad horizontal glow bands, and soft bloom—no letters, numbers, logos, diagrams, or readable interface.

### Kinesis — Sean Patel-Gould
- **Source:** https://seanpatelgould.com/portfolio/kinesis
- **Steal:** Pair a controlled cool lab ambience and sparse specular highlights with tactile, slightly warm practical dressing so the future-facing set remains human rather than sterile. The cool palette, specular highlights, retro-futurist material language, and balance of precision with warmth are stated by the production designer; applying one temperature to each host half is my inference.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** A cool source on one outer side and a warm source on the other gives the two 50/50 crops an identity even in a weak render, while broad highlights remain visible under constraints 3 and 4. A coherent, limited material language also prevents the generated background from competing with the centre card.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** A converted office lab feels precise but lived-in: cool cyan ambience brushes one side, a warm amber practical pools on the other, and only a few broad highlights catch brushed metal, moulded plastic, and painted plywood. Keep each colour source visibly motivated and confined to its own outer half, with neutral darkness through the middle.

### Developing a Small TV Studio Design — TVsetdesigns.com
- **Source:** https://tvsetdesigns.com/blogs/news/developing-a-small-tv-studio-design
- **Steal:** Put the desk and hosts forward, pull the background away, reveal a sliver of side wall rather than facing a flat wall square-on, and let the rear plane soften and darken. The source explicitly recommends talent-to-background distance, an oblique angle with a visible side wall, background blur to imply depth, and avoiding high-contrast background branding.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** Perspective lines and coarse light/dark layers create depth that survives constraint 3, while a soft, unbranded rear plane meets constraints 4 and 5. **Visual inference:** aim the perspective lines toward the outer edges, not the seam, so the centre remains quiet beneath the card.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** The shallow office set is staged on a slight angle rather than flat to the back wall; visible side returns, a foreground desk edge, a midground shelf, and a dim softened rear wall create four clear depth planes. Perspective and brighter accents pull toward the outer thirds, leaving the central vertical zone plain, dark, and low-contrast.

### The Basics of Film Lighting — Rosco
- **Source:** https://spectrum.rosco.com/the-basics-of-film-lighting
- **Steal:** Show a warm desk lamp on one side and a cool monitor or window-like source on the other, then imply larger off-camera lights matching each practical's direction and colour; add restrained back/rim light to separate both hosts. The source defines practical, motivated, three-point, and backlight techniques and notes that screen glow alone is rarely enough; this specific asymmetric scheme is my inference.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** Plausible opposing sources make the two halves distinguishable and a backlight preserves host silhouettes under constraint 3. Broad soft light and controlled spill avoid fragile gradients under constraint 4; blank practicals avoid constraint 5.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** Camera-left is shaped by a visible amber task lamp and matching soft warm key; camera-right is shaped by blank blue-green tube screens and a matching cool key. A restrained neutral rim separates both figures from the dark set, with flags implied to prevent either colour from washing across the quiet centre.

### SONOS Studio London — Brinkworth
- **Source:** https://www.designboom.com/architecture/sonos-studio-sound-bites-brinkworth-shoreditch-london-09-27-2015/
- **Steal:** Preserve the converted workplace shell—aggregate concrete, old industrial windows, reclaimed timber, acoustic curtains and movable vertical louvres—then layer individually dimmable domestic lamps over restrained architectural lighting. Those materials, acoustic devices, and two-scale lighting system are stated by the source; adapting them to read as a startup office is my inference.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** Broad timber, concrete, curtain, and louvre shapes read at constraint 3's small sizes, and individually separated pools of light produce robust contrast under constraint 4. It answers the “studio inside an office” premise without signs or logos, satisfying constraint 5.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** A scrappy converted tech office retains a concrete column, black-framed industrial glazing, reclaimed timber, and broad acoustic curtains, with movable vertical baffles doubling as set texture. Small domestic task lamps sit inside a restrained architectural lighting scheme, making the room feel inhabited rather than purpose-built.

### Compulsive Productions office — Matt Gibson Architecture + Design
- **Source:** https://www.designboom.com/architecture/matt-gibson-studio-office-compulsive-productions-06-18-2014/
- **Steal:** Use one or two open-ended plywood pods and faceted wall returns to frame long perspectives, while painting the unchanged office perimeter near-black so existing clutter recedes; punctuate depth with a few vertical stick lights. The source states that the dark perimeter hid existing components economically, and that open pods, faceted planes, old equipment, and suspended lights create depth; keeping the centre empty is my inference.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** A few large faceted planes and vertical bars survive constraints 3 and 4 better than dense dressing, while the shadowed office shell supports foreground separation. **Visual inference:** place pods and sticks only in the outer thirds, making the empty dark centre a deliberate negative-space channel for the OBS card.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** Two open-ended plywood work pods sit against a near-black existing office shell, their broad angled returns creating clear perspective without ornate detail. Sparse vertical tube lights and one or two pieces of obsolete equipment mark the outer thirds; the centre stays as uninterrupted shadow and matte wall.

### Glass Animals “Tour of the Earth” 2024 — 80six
- **Source:** https://www.80-six.com/case-studies/glassanimalstour
- **Steal:** Borrow the hierarchy of a few small CRT-shaped screens embedded as scenic objects within a larger architecture, but reduce the source's sixteen mini monitors and twenty-three synchronized surfaces to two small side clusters. The source states the counts, embedded mini-CRT format, and coordination among video, set, and lighting; the drastic reduction is my inference for Runtime.
- **Why it serves Runtime:** Repeated rounded rectangles communicate analogue broadcast hardware as silhouettes at constraint 3's 480p target; using only broad, asynchronous glow states avoids encoder-sensitive detail under constraint 4 and all readable content under constraint 5. Side clusters strengthen the warm/cool split while preserving the centre card's quiet field.
- **Prompt-safe wording:** Small clusters of thick rounded tube monitors are embedded into the far-left and far-right scenic walls as physical objects, never as a full video wall. Each glass face carries only a single broad field—dim snow, a rolling brightness band, or a blank coloured glow—and the centre wall contains no screens.

## Avoid

- **The giant seamless newsroom video wall.** Devlin Design Group's KYW set is a strong real broadcast reference (https://ddgtv.com/kyw-cbs-philadelphia-pa-debuts-new-audience-focused-set-from-devlin-design-group/), but its 23-foot curved LED wall and screen-bearing desk make live graphics the architecture. In Runtime that would generate flickering pseudo-text, violate constraint 5, and fight the deterministic centre card.
- **The full arena-control-room spectacle.** The 80six case study successfully coordinates 23 surfaces, including 16 mini-CRT-style monitors, but copying that density would turn the background into high-frequency noise after a half-frame crop and Twitch encode. Take the monitor silhouette, not the quantity.
- **Microscopic CRT authenticity.** The SLV Console's RGB shadow-mask shader, tiny labels, scratches, and fine edge wear reward 4K inspection; at 480p they will alias, vanish, or mutate between clips. Use one broad scan band, glass bloom, thick bezels, and large wear patches instead.
- **A bright object, lamp, or vanishing point on the centre seam.** Symmetrical “mission control” compositions look tempting in a wide two-shot, but Runtime covers that exact area with a card and crops on both sides of it. **Inference:** symmetry would also make small framing drift conspicuous because the seam becomes a ruler.
- **Unmotivated teal-and-orange wash.** Colouring every surface cyan on one side and orange on the other reads as a grade, not a room, and can muddy silhouettes under compression. Tie amber to a visible lamp and cool light to blank CRT glass or an off-frame office window, then keep the middle neutral.
